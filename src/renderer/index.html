Already linked Spotify to Discord?

Then Discord shows Spotify by itself. Turn Spotify off here so you don't appear twice, or unlink Spotify in Discord → Settings → Connections and let TuneCord handle everything.

SoundCloud or YouTube Music in a browser?

On Windows and Mac a browser tab shows up as "Chrome", "Edge" or "Firefox". Type SoundCloud in the "Show as" box next to it. On Linux TuneCord recognises the site by itself.

        How do I get an Application ID? (2 minutes)
        1. Open discord.com/developers/applications and sign in.
        2. Click New Application. The name you pick is what appears after "Listening to", e.g. Music.
        3. Copy the Application ID on the General Information page and paste it above.
        4. Optional: upload an app icon there. That's all, no bot or secret needed.
