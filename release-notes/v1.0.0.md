## TuneCord 1.0.0: first release 🎧

Show what you're listening to as your **Discord status**: Spotify, Apple Music, TIDAL, SoundCloud, Deezer, YouTube Music, Amazon Music and more. **No need to link Spotify to Discord**, and no Discord login. Just keep the Discord app open.

### What you get
- 🎧 A real **"Listening to"** status: the member list shows *Listening to M83* (or the song, or the app name).
- 🖼️ **Album art**, a **progress bar** that moves by itself, and a clickable song and artist.
- 🔘 Up to **2 buttons** for your friends, like *Open in TIDAL* or *Listen on Spotify*.
- ✍️ **Presence designer** with a live Discord-style preview: write your own lines with `{title}`, `{artist}`, `{album}` and more.
- ⏸️ Choose what happens when you pause: show it as paused, keep it, or hide it.
- 🧭 **Sources**: pin a player, hide players (e.g. Spotify if you already linked it), or rename a browser to "SoundCloud".
- 🙈 **Filter** for artists or words that should never show up.
- 🎛️ Music controls, global hotkeys, tray menu and a mini player.
- 📱 **Phone remote** via QR code, and 📊 **listening stats** (stored only on your computer).
- 🧪 **Send test** button to check that everything works without playing music.

### Works with
- **Windows 10/11**: everything that shows up in the Windows media flyout (Spotify, Apple Music, TIDAL, Deezer, Amazon Music, browsers, foobar2000, MusicBee…).
- **macOS**: Spotify and Apple Music. Install `media-control` (`brew install media-control`) for TIDAL, browsers and more.
- **Linux**: every MPRIS player. SoundCloud, TIDAL and YouTube Music browser tabs are recognised automatically.
