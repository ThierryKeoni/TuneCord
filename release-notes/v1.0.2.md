## TuneCord 1.0.2

### Fixed
- 🪟 **Windows: music not detected at all on some PCs.** TuneCord reads what's playing through a small PowerShell helper. On PCs where scripts are blocked (company policy, Intune/Group Policy, or antivirus blocking scripts in the temp folder) that helper couldn't start, so no player showed up, including desktop apps like TIDAL, Spotify and Apple Music. TuneCord now notices this and automatically starts the helper a second way that isn't affected.

### Improved
- 🔍 Helper problems now appear in Settings → Activity (starting, running, errors, and why it stopped), so you can see straight away what's going on.
