## TuneCord 1.0.4

### Fixed
- 🖼️ **Wrong album covers on Discord.** Windows players hand over the cover as an image file, and Discord only shows covers from the web, so TuneCord looks the song up online. It used to take the first search result, which was often a cover version, karaoke track, compilation or another album. Now it searches Deezer and Apple's catalogue and only accepts a cover when the artist, title and (when known) album and song length really match. No convincing match? You get the TuneCord logo instead of a wrong cover.
- 🔁 **Previous song's cover in the app.** Right after a song change, Windows sometimes still hands out the old cover. TuneCord now waits for the new one.
